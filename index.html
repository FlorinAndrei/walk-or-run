How this is computed
  • Drop sizes follow the Marshall–Palmer distribution for the chosen rain rate, split into 29 size classes from 0.2 to 6 mm. Fall speeds use the Atlas et al. (1973) fit. The distribution is rescaled so the ground-level flux equals the rain rate exactly.
  • Wind carries every drop sideways at the wind speed.
  • Seen from the body, all drops of one size travel in one direction. The water that class delivers is its water content × relative speed × the area of the body visible from that direction.
  • Visible area is measured on the GPU: the mannequin is rendered from 1,200 directions with a triangle ID per pixel, and pixels are counted per triangle. Limbs shadowing each other is handled by the depth buffer.
  • Not modelled: gait motion, splashes from the ground, runoff and clothing absorption, air flow around the body, gusts.
